Database and security reconciliation

Current read-only local catalog contains 17 application tables under familyconnect, no public social tables, and zero storage buckets. people contains identity/name/gender/age-class/photo/creator fields; it has no biography, birthday, timeline or album columns. Social tables are not simply hidden behind a missing frontend route.

The previous hosted read-only assessment recorded three public tables and two Auth accounts. That is historical evidence, not a fresh hosted inspection today. The legacy HTML points to a different historical Supabase project from the current FamilyConnect project. Original posts/photos, if any, may have lived there; their existence, retention and recoverability remain unknown. Do not infer data loss or emptiness from the current project's schema. No hosted project was contacted in this audit.

The repository migration chain assumes social tables already exist, with two competing comments/reactions naming schemes and no recovered complete social baseline. Do not apply it to the normalized database. It also includes authenticated-wide post/event access, an unrestricted invitation policy, and incomplete cross-family checks. Those rules must not be copied.

The historical Next.js app/api/profiles/route.js invokes a service-role helper for GET/POST without an authentication or membership check in that route. Do not restore that endpoint. Legacy raw HTML interpolation and public media URLs also require correction before reconnecting UI.

Recommended normalized connections — design only

  1. Posts: network ownership plus author Auth user and, where appropriate, author person. Validate person_accounts ownership and network membership; people.id is not auth.uid(). Keep about-person associations separate from authorship.
  2. Comments/reactions: reference a post and account; derive visibility from the post's authorized network and moderation state. Enforce uniqueness/ownership in the database, not just UI.
  3. Media: durable asset records with network/owner/object key, private bucket policies and authorized signed delivery. Minor/guardian rules and deletion must cover both metadata and storage objects.
  4. Profiles/date facts/events: extend person facts separately from account identity, with visibility, verification and partial-date support. Reuse the current relationship engine and approved graph; no parallel lineage model.
  5. Timeline/Home: authorized projections of posts/events/person facts, with pagination. Do not treat pending relationship suggestions as confirmed milestones or automatically publish birthday facts.
  6. Messaging: later, explicit conversations, participants and messages. Profile-sharing consent does not imply feed, gallery or message access across networks.

Recommended order after review: selectively port the existing text-feed UI against a tested local social adapter; then comments/reactions, private media and approved event/date projections. Personal timelines/galleries and messaging need separately scoped implementation because their complete workflows were not found. This is a recommendation, not an applied migration or implementation authorization.
