Approved sequence

Personal invitation → Identity confirmation → Family connection reveal → Optional family building → Voluntary invitations.

  1. Personal invitation: show the consent-approved inviter display name/photo, public-safe network label and welcoming message. Keep recipient details and private family records protected. Opening the opaque link does not consume it.
  2. Identity confirmation: securely authenticate with Supabase, retain invitation continuation, and collect only the minimum name information required. Existing profile claims require verified account/email binding or review; the link alone is insufficient. Detailed family questions are not a prerequisite.
  3. Immediate reveal: show the inviter, recipient and a readable branch using the existing graph, relationship finder and SVG renderer. Highlight a relationship path only when every required record is verified and authorized. Otherwise explain that the connection is incomplete and offer one optional question; skipping it still permits authorized exploration.
  4. Optional builder: ask “Let's grow your family tree! Who would you like to add first?” Offer parents, brothers or sisters, children, another relative, and skip. Ask only relevant questions. Sibling count is optional; individual names can be added incrementally.
  5. Voluntary invitations: after adding an adult, offer a personalized invitation via SMS, WhatsApp, email, native share or copy link. No automatic sends, contact uploads or invitation requirement.

Guided Family Builder

Keep the builder available from the dashboard after onboarding. Store drafts and progress automatically against the authenticated account and network, with visible saving/error status and idempotent writes. Resume the last saved step after leaving and returning. Do not cache private graph responses or invitation tokens in the PWA service worker.

Search only authorized existing records before proposing a new person. Offer potential matches for confirmation; never merge on a name alone. Enforce claim uniqueness transactionally and return privacy-safe conflict responses. Unresolved duplicates become review items rather than silently creating another claimed identity.

Support full, half, adoptive and step sibling descriptions without inventing shared parents. Parent/child additions record the proposed parentage type. Step or cousin assertions remain claims until supported by appropriate underlying records. The verified branch uses the existing engine; provisional people and connections appear in a clearly separate pending section of the live preview and do not enter confirmed relationship calculations.

Unknown age is not treated as adult. Invitation offers require an adult classification and appropriate permissions; minors remain guardian-managed and restricted. New profiles default to restricted visibility. Photo publication and cross-network sharing require explicit consent.
